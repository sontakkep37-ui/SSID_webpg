Interior design is the art and science of enhancing the interior of a building to create a healthier, more functional, and aesthetically pleasing environment. It involves planning and executing projects by selecting decorative items, colors, lighting, and materials, while also considering building codes, safety, and accessibility. The ultimate goal is to create spaces that meet the needs of the people using them. Functionality and aesthetics: A core principle is balancing practicality with visual appeal, ensuring a space is both beautiful and serves its intended purpose. Space planning: This involves determining how a space will be used and arranging elements like furniture and decor to optimize the layout, considering both filled (positive) and empty (negative) space. Technical knowledge: Professionals need a broad skill set, including understanding materials, textiles, color theory, and lighting. They must also be aware of structural requirements, health and safety regulations, and building codes. Material selection: Designers choose materials based on their function, durability, and impact on the environment, including effects on indoor air quality. Project management: Interior designers coordinate with contractors, architects, and other professionals to manage the project from planning through execution.
